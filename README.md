## Core Proposition

GDP measures what an economy produces.

HGDP proposes to measure the verified human value that an economy releases back to humanity.

The purpose of HGDP is not to immediately replace GDP, but to establish an experimental framework for measuring human time, energy, capability, knowledge, resource restoration, and other forms of verified human value generated or released by economic and technological systems.

## Evidence Principle

Potential Value ≠ Verified Value.

Creation Date ≠ Value Realisation Date.

Device Energy ≠ Social Energy.

Quantitative claims must not be presented as verified unless they have been measured and appropriately verified.

## Research Status

HGDP is a proposed economic theory and experimental framework.

It is not presented as an officially accepted replacement for GDP, national accounts, or existing statistical standards.

The research program is open to criticism, falsification, alternative measurement methods, replication, and independent research.
